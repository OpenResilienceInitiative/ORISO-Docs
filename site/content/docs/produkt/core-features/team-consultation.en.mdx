A new enquiry often raises a question for the team first: who has experience with this topic, who
has time, does anything look urgent? Counsellors need a place to talk about it — and that place must
never be the client's chat, not even hidden. ORISO gives every open enquiry its own team discussion:
an internal room next to the enquiry, in which only counsellors write.

This page describes **what** the feature does. It is written for counsellors and heads of
Beratungsstellen.

## 4.14.1 What you can do with it

- **Coordinate on a new enquiry** before anyone accepts it, without writing into the client's chat.
- **Always know which side you are on.** The panel carries a permanent "team only — invisible to the
  person seeking help" marker and shows how many posts there are.
- **Rely on a clean end.** When the enquiry is accepted, the discussion closes; after 90 days it is
  deleted.

## 4.14.2 How it works

| Phase | Team discussion |
|---|---|
| Enquiry is open | a separate room exists next to the enquiry; counsellors who can see the enquiry can write in it |
| Enquiry is accepted | the room is closed at once and archived; it is not carried into the case |
| After acceptance | the archive can be read, but nobody can write in it any more |
| 90 days after archiving | room and records are deleted |

After acceptance, the team uses the tools made for that phase: [Supervision](/en/produkt/core-features/session-management)
to accompany a case read-only, or [Case Handover](/en/produkt/core-features/case-handover) to look in or take
over. Three separate tools, each with a clear purpose.

<Callout>
The client is never a member of the team discussion. It is a room of its own, not a hidden part of
the client's chat — in an encrypted chat, a message cannot be hidden from one member.
</Callout>

## 4.14.3 Who takes part

Exactly the counsellors who can see the enquiry, and could therefore accept it, may take part. There
is no extra permission. The feature applies to counselling through a Beratungsstelle; the anonymous
live chat has no team discussion. A Träger can switch the feature off.

## 4.14.4 State of development

| Part | State |
|---|---|
| Open team Beratungsstelle in which every counsellor saw every case | existed before ORISO; replaced by team discussion and case handover |
