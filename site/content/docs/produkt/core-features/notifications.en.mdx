Counsellors decide for themselves what they see and hear, and when. Clients get only the
notifications they need, worded so that nobody looking over their shoulder learns anything about
their counselling. Notifications are the layer that makes both possible: one place in the app
where every event appears, plus e-mail, browser pop-ups and sounds that each person sets up.

This page describes **what** the feature does. It is written for counsellors, heads of
Beratungsstellen and Träger administrators.

## 4.8.1 What you can do with it

- **See every event in one place.** The activity timeline lists new enquiries, messages, replies
  in threads, handovers, calls, supervision and system events. It can be filtered by kind and
  searched, and each entry opens the conversation it belongs to.
- **Choose what reaches you.** For enquiries, conversations and time-critical events (calls and
  appointments) a counsellor sets, per kind of event, whether a browser pop-up appears, which
  sound plays and at what volume, and whether an e-mail is sent.
- **Pick a sound or silence it.** Each kind of event can have its own sound — twelve numbered
  tones plus a few named ones such as a ring tone — or none at all.
- **Have quiet time.** Do-not-disturb silences pop-ups, sounds and push messages on every device
  until a chosen time. The timeline keeps filling, so nothing is lost.
- **Get e-mails in your own language.** E-mails come in the person's language and form of address
  (German with "Sie" or "du", English, French, Russian, Turkish, Tigrinya).

## 4.8.2 Two audiences, two short lists

Clients and counsellors use ORISO very differently. A client uses it a few times, in a situation
they did not choose, sometimes on a shared phone. A counsellor works in it every day. ORISO
therefore keeps two separate lists of e-mail switches instead of one list filtered by role.

| | **Clients** | **Counsellors** |
|---|---|---|
| Switches | new message · appointment · service notice | new enquiry · daily overview · new message · assignment · handover · feedback · service notice |
| What an e-mail says | never names a counsellor, a case or any content | may name the event more specifically |
| Never switchable | account access, legal notices, outage notices | the same three |

Every notification e-mail carries a link that leads directly to the switch for exactly that
e-mail. Mails about account access and legal matters have no unsubscribe link, and the settings
screen says why.

<Callout>
A browser pop-up shows no message text unless the person turns that on. In counselling, a preview
on a lock screen is a privacy risk, so the default is off.
</Callout>

## 4.8.3 Who sets what

| Level | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Counsellor** | sets pop-up, sound, volume and e-mail per kind of event; sets do-not-disturb; switches sounds off entirely |
| **Client** | switches the e-mails meant for clients on or off; sets browser pop-ups |
| **Platform, Träger and Beratungsstelle** | decide per conversation type (agency counselling, live chat, self-help) whether clients may receive e-mails and browser notifications at all, and whether counsellors get e-mails about internal group chats — see [Feature permissions](/en/produkt/core-features/feature-permissions) |
| **Platform operator** | sends service notices, for example about planned maintenance; a notice is a draft until its author confirms it |

The personal settings are stored with the person's chat account, so they apply on every device
and browser they use.

## 4.8.4 What the server knows

The server stores an event as a type, a few reference numbers and the time — never the text that
is shown. The words in the timeline are produced in the person's own app, in their language. A
message preview is decrypted on the device; the server never sees message content.

## 4.8.5 How long notifications are kept

| What | Kept for |
|---|---|
| a timeline entry that has been read | 90 days |
| any timeline entry, read or not | at most 365 days |
| the entries of a deleted account | deleted with the account |

Both periods are the proposal from the data protection impact assessment and are configurable per
installation. They still await confirmation by the client's data protection officer.
