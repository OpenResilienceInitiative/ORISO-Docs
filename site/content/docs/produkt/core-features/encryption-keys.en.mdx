Counselling messages are end-to-end encrypted: only the people in a conversation can read them, not
the server and not the platform operator. The hard part is keeping them readable for their owner — on
a new device, after a password change, after a long absence. ORISO backs up the keys that unlock the
history, encrypted, so the person can always get back in.

This page describes **what** the feature does. It is written for counsellors, Träger administrators
and data protection officers.

## 4.16.1 What you can do with it

- **Keep reading your history after a password change** or on a new device.
- **Choose how strongly your history is protected:** the platform decides per role whether the
  history is unlocked with the login password or with a separate recovery key.
- **Save a recovery key** when the platform asks for one, and get reminded if you have not saved it.

## 4.16.2 How the history stays readable

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Key backup** | the keys for every conversation are stored on the server, encrypted; the server cannot read them |
| **Unlocking** | the backup is unlocked with the login password or with a recovery key, depending on the role |
| **Signed devices** | the devices of one account are signed as belonging together (cross-signing), so they can trust each other |
| **Restore** | on a new device, or after a password change, the history is restored from the backup |

## 4.16.3 Password or recovery key, per role

The platform administration decides, separately for people seeking help and for counsellors,
how **new** accounts unlock their history:

| Choice | Means | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| **Login password** | nothing extra to keep; whoever knows the password can read the history | people seeking help, who often use ORISO only a few times |
| **Recovery key** | a separate key the person stores safely; the password alone does not unlock the history | counsellors, for a higher level of protection |

A higher level of protection can thus be switched on with one setting, without new development and
without overburdening people seeking help.

## 4.16.4 Two optional protection levels

Two further levels come from the Element project, the reference app for Matrix. Both are built and
are switched on per installation:

- **Readable after an absence.** Messages that arrive while a person is logged out are still
  readable when they log in again.
- **Keys only for signed devices.** Keys for a conversation go only to devices that are signed as
  belonging to the account. An unknown device receives nothing it could decrypt.

Calls are encrypted end to end as well: the video server only forwards encrypted media.

## 4.16.5 State of development

| Part | State |
|---|---|
