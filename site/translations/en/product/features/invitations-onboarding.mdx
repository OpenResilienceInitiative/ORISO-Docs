A Träger that starts on ORISO has to invite many people at once: its own administrators, the heads
of its Beratungsstellen and their counsellors. Each of them has to create an account, set up a
second factor and, for the Träger, conclude the contract documents. When this happens by e-mail
and phone, invitations get lost. ORISO follows every invitation until the account is ready and
shows where it is stuck.

This page describes **what** the feature does. It is written for Träger administrators and heads
of Beratungsstellen.

## 4.12.1 What you can do with it

- **See where each invitation stands** — and why one is stuck, for example because the e-mail
  could not be delivered or the link has expired.
- **Set up a Träger with a wizard** — account, second factor and contract documents, step by step.
- **Invite many at once** — several new Beratungsstellen with their heads, counsellors and topic
  rights in one round, or from a spreadsheet (CSV).
- **Let counsellors set themselves up** — choose topics, or create a new Beratungsstelle while
  setting up; heads decide whether they also counsel themselves.

## 4.12.2 The stages of an invitation

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Prepared** | the invitation is written but not sent yet |
| **Invited** | the e-mail with the link has gone out |
| **Account created** | the person has created their account |
| **Done** | everything the role needs is set up |
| **Needs action** | something blocks the next step; the reason is shown |

The steps differ by role. A Träger administrator additionally goes through creating the Träger and
the contract documents; a counsellor goes through invited, account created, done.

An invitation into a Beratungsstelle that does not exist yet waits — unsent and without a link that
could expire — until that Beratungsstelle's first head has set it up. If no head is invited, the
administration says so.

## 4.12.3 Who invites whom

| Role | Can invite |
|---|---|
| **Platform operator** | Träger administrators |
| **Träger administrator** | heads of Beratungsstellen and counsellors of its own Träger; several new Beratungsstellen at once |
| **Head of Beratungsstelle** | counsellors of their own Beratungsstelle |

Invitation e-mails can be written in your own words with an e-mail template, with placeholders and
a preview before sending. Who may be invited into which Beratungsstelle
always stays within the inviting person's own Träger.

## 4.12.4 Set-up after accepting

- **Träger administrators** go through the set-up wizard: organisation details, second factor,
  contract documents. Without signed contract documents no Beratungsstelle can be created.
- **Heads of Beratungsstellen** set up their Beratungsstelle and decide whether they also counsel.
- **Counsellors** confirm or choose their topics, in the language they prefer.

## 4.12.5 State of development

| Part | State |
|---|---|

| Träger wizard (account, second factor, contract documents) | commissioned with CAR-14 and built |
