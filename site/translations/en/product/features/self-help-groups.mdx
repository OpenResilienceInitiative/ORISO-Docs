title: "4.13 Self-help Groups"
description: "Protected group rooms for self-help: joining only after the group lead admits you, a waiting area with countdown and group rules, handing over the group lead, joining by link without an account, and recurring group appointments."
---

Self-help needs protected rooms. Nobody should walk into a group unasked, and a group must not stop
because its lead is away. ORISO's self-help groups build on the group chat: a person asks to join,
waits in a waiting area that shows the group's rules, and is admitted by the lead. The lead can be
handed over, and the group can meet at regular times.

This page describes **what** the feature does. It is written for counsellors who lead groups and
for heads of Beratungsstellen.

## 4.13.1 What you can do with it

- **Admit people yourself.** A person asks to join; the group lead admits or declines.
- **Prepare people before they enter.** The waiting area shows a countdown to the start and the
  group's rules.
- **Hand over the lead.** The main lead can pass the group to another counsellor.
- **Let people join without an account.** With a group link, a person can join for this one
  occasion without registering.
- **Meet regularly.** Plan recurring group appointments with reminder e-mails.

## 4.13.2 How joining works

| Step | What the person sees | What the lead does |
|---|---|---|
| Open the group link | the group's information and its rules | — |
| Ask to join | a waiting area with a countdown to the start | sees the request and admits or declines it |
| Admitted | the group room; messages are end-to-end encrypted and visible only to members | — |
| Declined or not answered | stays outside; nothing of the group is visible | — |

Only members read the group. A person who has not been admitted sees neither messages nor other
members.

## 4.13.3 Joining without an account

A person can join through a group link with "join without account". The login then exists only in
this one browser. Once the longest possible login has ended, nobody can use that account again, and
it is deleted after 24 hours. This is a technical limit, not a legal retention period. What the
person wrote stays visible to the members who were present.

## 4.13.4 Who does what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Group lead (owner)** | admits or declines requests; sets the rules; hands the lead over to another counsellor |
| **Co-moderator** | supports the lead in the group |
| **Participant** | asks to join, reads and writes once admitted, leaves at any time |
| **Träger and Beratungsstelle** | switch self-help groups on or off, see [Feature permissions](/product/features/feature-permissions) |

## 4.13.5 Group appointments

A group can be planned as a series: daily, weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly or yearly,
with a fixed number of meetings. A meeting can be text, audio or video. Participants and leads
receive e-mails when an appointment is confirmed, moved or cancelled, and a reminder beforehand.
Calendar entries and invitations name no topic, so they reveal nothing about what the group is
about.

## 4.13.6 State of development

| Part | State |
|---|---|
| Creating, starting, ending and joining a group chat; single appointments and calendar file | existed before ORISO |
| Group chat for further groups of people, including among counsellors | commissioned with CAR-REFACT and built |
| Join request with admission, waiting area, rules, handing over the lead, joining without an account | built |
| Recurring group appointments with reminder e-mails | built; requested, not priced in an offer |
