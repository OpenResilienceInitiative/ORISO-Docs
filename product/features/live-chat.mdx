Some people only write when they do not have to give their name. The live chat lets a person talk
to a counsellor straight away, anonymously and without registering. They pick a topic, get a
pseudonym, wait in a waiting room that shows their place in the queue, and are taken on by the next
available counsellor. Afterwards, the anonymous access is switched off and deleted.

This page describes **what** the feature does. It is written for counsellors, heads of
Beratungsstellen and data protection officers.

## 4.2.1 What you can do with it

As a person seeking help:

- start a chat without an account and see how many people are ahead of you
- do a short breathing exercise in the waiting room and read how the chat works
- see and confirm a pseudonym with an animal avatar before the chat starts
- get a first response straight away, in your own language
- leave the queue at any time; your access is then switched off

As a counsellor:

- switch your own availability on and off; the waiting room never shows who is available
- take on the next person from the queue and talk in an end-to-end encrypted room

As a Träger:

- offer live chat for any topic area, through links created in the administration
- decide in which conversation types calls, uploads and other functions are allowed, see
  [Feature permissions](/product/features/feature-permissions)

## 4.2.2 The way through

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Entry** | the person opens a live-chat link, picks a topic and receives a generated pseudonym |
| **Consent 1** | agreement to the privacy policy of the platform (or of the Träger, if the link already belongs to one) |
| **Waiting room** | position in the queue, breathing exercise, explanation of the chat; counsellor availability is not shown |
| **Accepted** | a counsellor takes the person on; only now is a Beratungsstelle responsible |
| **Consent 2** | in the room, before the first message: agreement to the Beratungsstelle's text |
| **Conversation** | end-to-end encrypted chat; the first response appears as a short sequence of messages |
| **End** | the person or the counsellor ends the chat; the anonymous access is switched off |

There are two consents because two different bodies are responsible. In the waiting room, no
Beratungsstelle is assigned yet, so only the platform's (or Träger's) text can apply. The
Beratungsstelle's text applies once a counsellor has taken the person on. See
[Legal texts](/product/features/legal-texts).

## 4.2.3 The first response

When a person has written, they immediately get a first response in their language: what happens
next, that they should not send personal data, emergency numbers, and — where it applies — an offer
to save their access or leave an e-mail address. The platform defines the order; a Träger can add
its own wording. The response is stored once, as one event, so it can be shown later exactly as it
was said. It comes from the platform, not from a person or a bot in the room; no additional reader
is added to the encrypted conversation.

## 4.2.4 Anonymity and deletion

The person is anonymous up to the point of entering the chat: the display name is generated and
cannot be changed, the password is generated too. What they then write is up to them.

| What | When |
|---|---|
| conversation ended and anonymous access switched off | at once when the person leaves, or after 6 hours without any activity |
| anonymous account and conversation deleted | 47 hours after the last activity of an ended conversation (configurable per installation) |

The offer named 3 hours for deletion; the current setting is 47 hours. The final period is still to
be agreed with Caritas.
