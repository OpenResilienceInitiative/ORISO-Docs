## Flow boundaries to explain

The **platform-contract flow** concerns the platform operator and provider. The accepted contract decision names the full agreement, its annexes, conclusion and signature. The **legacy flow called DPA** manages versions, invitations, signing and status in the current contract-documents module. Its mapping to the agreement-and-annex model remains explicit: the existing name does not prove an annex-only model or delivery of the complete agreement.

The **help-seeker consent flow** is a separate workflow governed by the consent decisions. The **practice flow** is draft vocabulary from the supplied text and does not add permissions or legal exceptions.
